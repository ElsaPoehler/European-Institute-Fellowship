The Fellowship

What is the European Institute Fellowship?

The Fellowship is a programme of the EBS European Institute for students who want to engage with Europe's future beyond the lecture hall. Over one academic cycle, Fellows research central questions around the Draghi Report, derive concrete opportunities, challenges and innovation potential for Europe – and share their results with a wider audience.

The EBS European Institute

A forum for Europe's future.

The EBS European Institute addresses the most pressing questions facing Europe. It connects business, academia and institutions, aims to foster a shared European awareness and to help put the reform proposals of the Draghi and Letta reports into practice.

Visit the official Institute page →
Mission

The Institute brings together senior leaders from business and politics, studies key questions of the future, develops solutions with industry partners and feeds them directly into teaching across economics, law and politics. The aim: responsible, bold and visionary leaders for Europe.
